Our Professional Journey

A legacy of maritime & interior design excellence spanning over three decades.

2023 – Present

Disney Adventure & River Cruises

Project Manager & FF&E Coordinator for Disney Adventure across the entire ship. Managed FF&E coordination for AmaWaterways (8 river cruise ships) and Holland America refits.

2021 – 2023

Disney Cruise Line Fleet

Project Manager / Deputy PM for Disney Wish, Disney Treasure, and Disney Destiny. Directed crew areas, accommodation suites, and public spaces.

2020 – 2021

Enchanted Princess

Project Manager overseeing public areas, lounges, bars, retail spaces, and photo shops. Coordinated overall design management with Tillberg team and shipyards.

2018 – 2020

P&O Australia, Mein Schiff & Hurtigruten

Deputy Project Manager & Interior Designer. Managed design liaison between shipyard and clients. Designed lounges, restaurants, gyms, and spas for Mein Schiff and Hurtigruten fleet.

2014 – 2017

Tillberg Design of Sweden

Interior Designer across Sweden, Poland, and Malta. Led accommodation & public area designs for NCL Joy NewBuild, NCL Gem, NCL Epic, and NCL Sun refits.

2002 – 2014

Business Venture & Family

Co-founded and successfully managed an opticians business while raising two children, broadening management and client relations expertise.

1998 – 2001

Designteam London

Project Manager for Royal Caribbean International (Radiance of the Seas & Brilliance of the Seas). Managed retail areas, cafés, and photo galleries.

1994 – 1998

Tillberg Design / SMC Design

Interior Designer (Sweden & London). Designed accommodation areas for Costa Victoria and Star Cruises (Superstar Leo, Virgo, Scorpio). Created public areas (Bistro, Casino) for NCL Sky & Star, P&O Aurora, and RCI Explorer of the Seas.

1992 – 1994

Kusch Yacht Agency

Assistant Interior Designer. Specialized in converting 2 tug boats to luxury yachts at Malta Drydocks – Simpson's & Arctic P.
