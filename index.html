Our Professional Journey

A legacy of maritime & interior design excellence spanning over three decades.

2026

High-End Residential & Bespoke Architecture

Delivered detailed architectural and interior drawing packages for ultra-luxury private residences. Focused on custom joinery, refined material finishes, and flawless technical execution for demanding private clients.

2023 – Present

Disney Adventure, AmaWaterways & MSC Fleet

Led end-to-end design development and project management for mega-vessels and river cruise ships. Managed FF&E coordination for AmaWaterways (8 ships) and Holland America refits, alongside taking full ownership of signature onboard zones (SPA, specialty restaurants) and technical documentation for MSC Cruises.

2018 – 2023

Disney Cruise Line Fleet (Wish, Treasure, Destiny)

Co-led the interior design and delivery process for three Disney Cruise Line newbuild vessels across a 5-year development cycle. Directed multidisciplinary teams of up to 20 designers, taking total ownership of the luxury cabins, suites, and public spaces scope—from early concepts and mock-up approvals to shipyard execution.

2020 – 2021

Enchanted Princess

Directed project management and overall design coordination for public areas, luxury lounges, bars, and retail spaces, liaising directly with shipyard engineers and international stakeholders.

2017 – 2020

Ritz-Carlton Superyacht & Fleet Designs

Developed bespoke interior design packages and technical drawings for guest suites onboard the ultra-luxury Ritz-Carlton Superyacht as well as newbuild cruise liners. Designed high-end lounges, specialty dining, wellness spas, and gyms for Mein Schiff and Hurtigruten fleets.

2014 – 2017

Tillberg Design of Sweden & Commercial Interiors

Interior designers across Sweden, Poland, and Malta. Led accommodation and public space design for Norwegian Cruise Line (NCL Joy, Gem, Epic, Sun refits) and developed technical documentation for upscale hospitality and restaurant concepts.

2002 – 2014

Business Venture & Client Relations

Co-founded and successfully managed an opticians business while raising two children, broadening strategic management, client relations, and operational expertise.

1998 – 2001

Royal Caribbean International (Designteam London)

Project Management for Radiance of the Seas & Brilliance of the Seas. Overseeing design intent and execution for retail environments, cafés, and onboard public galleries.

1994 – 1998

SMC Design & Tillberg (London & Sweden)

Created public and accommodation interior spaces for Costa Victoria, Star Cruises (Leo, Virgo, Scorpio), NCL Sky & Star, P&O Aurora, and RCI Explorer of the Seas.

1992 – 1994

Kusch Yacht Agency (Malta Drydocks)

Specialized in luxury maritime conversions, transforming two commercial tug boats into iconic private superyachts – Simpson's & Arctic P.
