Our Professional Journey

A legacy of design excellence spanning over a decade before establishing our own studio.

2014

Early Foundations

Began our careers in boutique residential design, learning the intricacies of bespoke craftsmanship and client relations.

2015

First International Commission

Secured and successfully delivered our first overseas project: a luxury apartment in the heart of Milan.

2016

Entry into Maritime Design

Transitioned into the maritime sector as Junior Architects, adapting high-end aesthetics to strict engineering constraints.

2018

Senior Designers at Royal Maritime

Led the interior architecture for ultra-luxury cruise suites, focusing on bespoke joinery and optimizing spatial flow.

2019

Sustainable Luxury Award

Recognized for integrating sustainable, highly-engineered materials into high-end yachting environments without compromising aesthetics.

2024

Founding Curated Interiors
